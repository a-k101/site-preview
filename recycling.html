Recycling old electricals (WEEE)

Electrical items shouldn't go in your normal bin — that's what the crossed-out wheelie bin symbol on a device means. They should be recycled properly, so the materials are recovered and nothing harmful reaches landfill.

We'll take your old kit back

When we supply you a new device, we'll take the old equivalent one back and recycle it responsibly, free of charge — just ask.

Recycling it yourself

You can also drop old electricals at your local household waste recycling centre. Find your nearest point at [recycleyourelectricals.org.uk] or via your council's website.

Not sure what to do with an old device we supplied? Get in touch and we'll help.
