About us

Privacy used to be the default. Somewhere along the way it became a luxury — something you get only if you're wealthy enough to pay for it or technical enough to build it yourself. We think that's backwards.

Everyone deserves a private, secure digital life, explained in plain English and set up properly — not just big companies and tech experts. So that's what we do: we take the tools and know-how that normally sit behind a corporate IT department and bring them to individuals, families and small businesses — locally, personally, and honestly.

What we believe

  • Privacy is a right, not a luxury.It shouldn't take money or technical skill to keep your own life your own. Making that accessible is the whole job.
  • Honesty over hype.We'll tell you the truth — including the limits of what any technology can do, and the cheapest thing that actually solves your problem. If you don't need something, we'll say so.
  • Local and personal.You'll deal with a real person who lives near you and answers the phone — the same person who set everything up. Not a call centre, not a ticket queue, not a chatbot.
  • We practise what we preach.Your data stays yours. We work privacy-first and local-first — nothing sold, nothing shipped off to clouds in other countries, no black boxes. The security software we run, we built ourselves and keep on our own machines.
  • Small on purpose.We're deliberately small, so you're a name we know rather than an account number. Specialised, not spread thin.

A real person, not a call centre

Most security and IT companies keep you at arm's length — a helpdesk, a queue, a script. We're the opposite. One person turns up, understands your situation, sets things up properly, and is on the end of the phone when you need them — the same person every time, who already knows your setup and would rather explain something than baffle you with it.

That's not a small thing when you're trusting someone with your home network or your firm's confidential data. You should be able to reach a real human — and know they know their craft.

How it works

  • Get in touch. Tell us a little about your situation — no pressure, no jargon, no obligation.
  • We listen, and assess. We work out what you actually need — and we're honest if that turns out to be very little, or nothing at all.
  • We recommend, plainly. A clear plan and a straight price, pointing you to the simplest thing that solves the problem, not the most expensive.
  • We set it up properly. Done right the first time, configured for how you actually live or work, and explained so you understand it.
  • We look after it. With an optional care plan we keep everything running — often fixing things before you notice — and stay a phone call away.

Earning your trust, not borrowing it

We'd rather earn your confidence than claim it. So you won't find invented reviews here — just straight talk, work done properly, and a standing promise: we'll always tell you the truth about what you need and what it can do, even when a quick sale would be easier.
