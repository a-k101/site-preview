Questions, answered honestly

The things people most often ask us — with straight answers, including the limits.

The essentials

If you install a device in my home, how does that actually give me privacy?

Fair question — a box on your network should have to earn that trust. Everything in your home reaches the internet through this one box, which makes it the perfect place to stand guard. Three things happen there:

  • It blocks trackers before they ever connect. Ad and tracking companies rely on your devices quietly phoning home to them in the background. The box keeps a constantly-updated list of those trackers and simply refuses the connection — so they never get to log you. (Pages often load cleaner and faster as a bonus.)
  • It stops your browsing being handed over. Normally, for every site you visit, your device first asks a big company "where is this website?" — quietly handing them a diary of everywhere you go. Our box looks it up itself instead, so no single company gets that diary.
  • It filters what you want filtered. Adult content, scams and malware blocked for the whole house, with the children on their own protected network.

And the trust part, plainly: the box works for you, not for us. Your activity isn't sent to us, isn't sold, and never goes to any cloud — it stays in your home. It's built on well-known, respected privacy tools, not a mystery black box. The honest limit: it can't make you invisible — your broadband provider can still see which sites you connect to, and it can't follow your phone onto mobile data away from home. What it does is switch off the constant profiling built into normal home internet, and put you back in control of the rest.

Can I stop even my broadband provider from seeing which sites I visit?

Yes — that's exactly what a VPN is for, and we can set one up for you. The honest picture, because this corner of the world is full of hype: your broadband provider can normally see the name of every site you connect to. A VPN wraps all your traffic in an encrypted tunnel, so your provider sees only that the tunnel exists — not where you're going. The catch worth understanding: this doesn't make you invisible, it moves the trust. Instead of your broadband provider seeing your destinations, the VPN company could — so the only ones worth using are reputable providers with an independently-checked "no-logs" promise, and those are the only ones we'll recommend. We can set it up on your devices, or run it at your router so your whole home is covered. It's an optional extra on a small subscription with the provider.

Can you see my browsing, or keep any of my data?

No. Your activity isn't routed through us or stored in any cloud. The box keeps only minimal logs on your own equipment for troubleshooting, and nothing about where you go leaves your home.

Isn't this something I could just set up myself?

Some of it, if you've the time, patience and know-how. What you're really paying for is having it done properly the first time, kept working, and a real person to call when something's not right — instead of a weekend lost to forums.

Do I own the equipment?

Yes, completely. You buy it and it's yours; the optional care plan simply keeps it running.

What if something goes wrong, or you're not available?

Every setup is documented and backed up, so your protection never hinges on one person being reachable. On a care plan, we monitor things and often fix them before you'd even notice.

Your home & family

Will this really keep my children safe online?

It gives you the strongest, hardest-to-dodge protection on your home network — adult content, scams and malware blocked on every device, a separate kids' network, and bedtimes. We're honest, though: no filter is perfect, and nothing controls mobile data away from home. Proper protection, set up right — not a magic wall.

Can my tech-savvy teenager get around it?

Much harder than shop-bought parental controls, and the Advanced setup is tougher still. But we'll never promise a determined teenager can't find a way — we'll promise the strongest, best-maintained defence, and honest advice.

Do I need to be technical to use it?

Not at all. We set everything up, and you can call a real person whenever you need.

Can I have the same enterprise-grade protection you install for businesses, in my home?

Yes — and for some families it's genuinely the right call, not overkill. The most common reason is children. A consumer router can filter websites, but a determined child or teenager can often find a way around it. The professional firewall we use for regulated firms lets us put the kids on a truly separate network with much tighter, harder-to-bypass controls — blocking the common workarounds, not just the obvious sites. It's also the right fit if you work from home with sensitive data, your house is full of smart devices you'd like walled off, or you simply want the strongest possible control. We'll help you work out whether it's right for your home — we won't push you toward more than you need, but where it fits, and especially with children in the house, it's absolutely available and often well worth it.

Private phones (individuals)

What is GrapheneOS — is it hard to use?

It's a private, security-hardened version of Android that runs on Google Pixel phones. Day to day it feels like a normal phone; we set it up and show you how to use it.

Will I still have my normal apps and banking?

Almost always — calls, messages, maps, most apps and banking work fine. We'll be upfront about the occasional app that doesn't, and point you to good alternatives.

Do you supply the phone?

You buy the Pixel and own it outright; we install the private software and teach you to use it.

For business

What's the difference between your two business options?

The small-business option is one well-configured gateway that keeps threats out — right for an ordinary office. The regulated-firm option is an enterprise-grade firewall that also monitors traffic, walls off your network and secures remote staff — for firms handling confidential client data.
